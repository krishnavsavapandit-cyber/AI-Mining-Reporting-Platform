----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every technology, framework, engine, and library in the platform is audited and classified into
one of four maturity tiers:

[RED]    — BASIC (Level 1)
           Foundational utilities, standard third-party helper libraries, and routine dependencies
           used for basic functionality without extensive custom architectural wrapping.

[YELLOW] — INTERMEDIATE (Level 2)
           Standard industry frameworks, data manipulation libraries, and reliable operational
           infrastructure requiring proper configuration, pipelines, and baseline integration.

[GREEN]  — ADVANCED (Level 3)
           High-complexity components, strict type systems, multi-tiered security, high-accuracy
           extraction pipelines, hybrid retrieval algorithms, and automated test regression suites.

[BLUE]   — MASTER (Level 4)
           State-of-the-art architectural achievements, custom-designed multi-agent orchestration,
           zero-hallucination grounded reasoning, polymorphic database abstraction, and bespoke
           design system engineering representing elite engineering mastery.
